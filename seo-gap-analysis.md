## Executive Summary

This comprehensive SEO GAP analysis identifies critical optimization opportunities for Able Plumbing Inc.'s website based on Google My Business profile data, keyword research findings, and current website structure analysis. The analysis reveals significant gaps in page structure, title tag optimization, internal linking, and service coverage that are limiting the website's ability to rank for high-intent local search queries.

**Key Findings:**
- Missing dedicated pages for 6+ core services mentioned in keyword research
- Title tag structure does not follow local SEO best practices (missing city modifiers on most pages)
- Weak internal linking from homepage to key service categories
- Geographic coverage pages exist but lack service-specific depth
- No dedicated emergency plumbing landing page despite 24/7 business model
- Kitchen and bathroom plumbing mentioned but lack dedicated optimized pages
- Missing specialized service pages for high-value, low-competition keywords
