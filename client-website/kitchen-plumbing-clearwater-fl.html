Common Kitchen Plumbing Issues We Fix

  • Leaky Kitchen Faucets: Worn washers, O-rings, or cartridges causing drips and leaks
  • Clogged Kitchen Drains: Grease, food particles, and soap buildup blocking drains
  • Garbage Disposal Problems: Jammed, leaking, or not working disposals
  • Low Water Pressure: Clogged aerators or supply line issues reducing water flow
  • Leaking Under Sink: Damaged pipes, loose connections, or failed sink seals
  • Dishwasher Issues: Water not draining, leaks, or connection problems
  • Outdated Fixtures: Old, inefficient fixtures needing replacement
