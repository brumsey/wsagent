Advanced Leak Detection Technology

Infrared Thermal Imaging

Our infrared cameras detect temperature differences caused by water leaks, allowing us to find hidden leaks in walls, ceilings, and floors without invasive investigation.

Acoustic Leak Detection

Sonic listening devices amplify the sound of water escaping from pipes, helping us pinpoint the exact location of underground and slab leaks.

Video Camera Inspection

We insert waterproof cameras into pipes and sewer lines to visually inspect for cracks, breaks, and leak points.

Smoke Testing

For sewer line leaks and drain issues, we use non-toxic smoke to identify leak locations and connection problems.

Pressure Testing

We test water pressure in isolated sections of plumbing to confirm leak presence and severity before repair.

Signs You May Have a Hidden Leak

High Water Bills

Sudden increase in water costs without increased usage indicates a possible leak.

Running Water Sounds

Hearing water running when all fixtures are off suggests a hidden leak.

Wet Spots

Unexplained wet areas on floors, walls, or ceilings point to water leaks.

Mold or Mildew

Growth of mold or musty odors often indicates moisture from hidden leaks.

Foundation Cracks

New cracks in foundation or slab can be caused by slab leaks.

Low Water Pressure

Decreased water pressure may indicate a significant leak in your system.
