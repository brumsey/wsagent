Common Bathroom Plumbing Problems

  • Running Toilets: Flapper valve, fill valve, or overflow tube issues wasting water
  • Clogged Toilets: Persistent clogs requiring professional clearing
  • Leaky Shower Faucets: Dripping showerheads or faucets wasting water
  • Low Water Pressure: Clogged aerators or supply line restrictions
  • Slow Draining Tubs: Hair and soap buildup blocking bathroom drains
  • Toilet Won't Flush: Clogged trapway or broken flush mechanism
  • Water Leaks: Leaks under sinks, around toilets, or from shower valves
