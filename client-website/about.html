About Able Plumbing, Inc.

Combining Yesterday's Integrity with Today's Technology

Your Trusted Clearwater Plumber Since 1983

Able Plumbing, Inc. is a locally owned and operated plumbing company serving Clearwater, FL and the greater Tampa Bay area. Since receiving our Florida plumbing license in 1983, we've built a reputation for honest service, skilled craftsmanship, and unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction.

For over 24 years, we've been the plumbing company Tampa Bay families and businesses trust when they need reliable service—whether it's an emergency at 3 AM or a planned kitchen renovation.

Our Mission: Integrity Meets Innovation

Our mission is simple: combine traditional values of integrity and customer service with modern plumbing technology and methods. We believe in treating every customer like family while using the latest tools and techniques to solve plumbing problems efficiently and effectively.

What Makes Us Different

No-Commission Technicians

Unlike many plumbing companies, we pay our technicians by the hour—not by commission. This means our plumbers focus on solving your problems, not making sales. You get honest recommendations, not pressure to buy unnecessary services.

24/7/365 Availability

Plumbing emergencies don't wait for business hours. That's why we're available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year—including nights, weekends, and holidays. When you call, you reach a real person ready to help.

Advanced Technology

We invest in modern plumbing technology including infrared leak detection, video camera inspection, hydro jetting equipment, and trenchless sewer repair. Advanced tools mean faster diagnosis and better solutions.

Transparent Pricing

We provide upfront, honest pricing before we begin work. Our time-and-materials approach means you pay for actual work done, not inflated flat rates. No hidden fees, no surprises.

Licensed & Certified

We hold valid Florida plumbing license CFC026561 (since 1983) and construction license CBC1265498. All our technicians are highly trained, skilled professionals who take pride in their work.

Wide Service Area

We serve 60+ municipalities across Pinellas, Hillsborough, and Pasco Counties. As we like to say, "We're not afraid to travel." If you're in the Tampa Bay area, we'll be there.
