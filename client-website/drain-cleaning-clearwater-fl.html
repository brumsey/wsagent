Why Hydro Jetting is Superior

At Able Plumbing, we prefer hydro jetting over traditional drain snaking for most clogs. Here's why:

Complete Cleaning

Hydro jetting cleans the entire pipe diameter, removing grease, scale, and debris completely. Traditional snaking just pokes a hole through the clog.

Long-Lasting Results

By thoroughly cleaning pipes, hydro jetting prevents clogs from returning quickly. You'll have free-flowing drains for much longer.

Removes Tree Roots

High-pressure water cuts through tree roots that have invaded sewer lines—something traditional snaking can't do effectively.

Environmentally Friendly

Hydro jetting uses only water—no harsh chemicals that damage pipes or harm the environment.

Safe for Pipes

When performed by licensed professionals, hydro jetting is safe for all pipe materials and actually extends pipe life.

Signs You Need Drain Cleaning

Slow Drains

Water draining slowly indicates partial blockage building up in your pipes.

Frequent Clogs

Recurring clogs mean underlying buildup that needs professional cleaning.

Gurgling Sounds

Strange noises from drains indicate air trapped by blockages.

Bad Odors

Foul smells from drains suggest organic buildup or sewer gas escape.

Multiple Clogged Fixtures

Several drains clogged at once indicates main sewer line blockage.

Sewage Backup

Water or sewage backing up is a plumbing emergency requiring immediate attention.
