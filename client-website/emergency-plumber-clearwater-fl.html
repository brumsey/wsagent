What to Do During a Plumbing Emergency

For Burst Pipes or Major Leaks:

  • Shut off the main water valve immediately
  • Turn off electricity to affected areas if water is near outlets
  • Move valuables and furniture away from water
  • Place buckets under leaks if possible
  • Call (727) 330-2733 for emergency service

For Sewer Backups:

  • Stop using all water fixtures and toilets
  • Do not attempt to clear the backup yourself
  • Keep children and pets away from the area
  • Call us immediately for emergency sewer service

For Gas Leaks:

  • Evacuate the building immediately
  • Do not use light switches, phones, or anything that could create a spark
  • Call the gas company and 911 from a safe location
  • Call (727) 330-2733 once it's safe for emergency gas line repair

For No Hot Water:

  • Check if the pilot light is out (gas water heaters)
  • Check your circuit breaker (electric water heaters)
  • Look for signs of water leaking from the heater
  • Call us for emergency water heater diagnosis and repair
